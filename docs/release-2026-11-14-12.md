# Release 2026.11.14.12

Monthly settlement and dated study cessation.

- Add “تسوية” to monthly receipt types. Period dates, billable lesson count and agreed gross unit price are visible and editable. Default price is the applicable monthly fee divided by scheduled monthly lessons. Discounts follow the existing bearer and are scaled with the settled gross amount. The agreement changes only that month; subsequent months use regular monthly billing.
- Billing agreements are stored independently from cash receipts. Partial payments leave the real remaining debt. Staff can save an agreement without recording new cash, including a zero-lesson period. Deleting a receipt does not delete the approved billing agreement.
- Learner editing exposes study status, last included study date and stop reason. Stopping closes all current enrollments without deleting identity, receipts, attendance or past arrears. Future monthly dues and session recognition are excluded after the closing date.
- Prepaid canceled months remain cash and are identified as learner credit for review/refund, with no teacher or institution earned allocation. Previously paid teacher settlements require financial reconciliation before conflicting changes.
- Explicit later registration starts a new period and does not charge the stopped gap. Same-group periods in the same month cannot overlap. Historical dates remain accessible to attendance screens.
- Arrears use monthly allocations, including composite receipts and net refunds. Cloud loading and local saves share the same lifecycle and settlement calculations.

Validation: complete mocked DOM/auth/cloud integration suite with synthetic records. Tests cover midmonth starts, partial receipts, standalone agreements, discount allocation, teacher-payment safeguards, dated stops, past debt, future prepaid credits, reload consistency and a later enrollment gap. Existing attendance, cash, session ledger, privacy and atomic-save checks pass. No production records were changed by testing. Mobile visual acceptance remains a staff check.

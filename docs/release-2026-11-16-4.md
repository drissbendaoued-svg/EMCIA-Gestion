# Release 2026.11.16.4

Add configurable monthly institutional obligations for rent, utilities, internet and individually named staff salaries. Each obligation defines its first month, due day, optional amount and advance reminder days. Days beyond month-end clamp to the month's last day. Amount/due-day changes are versioned from a selected month; stopping applies after the current month and preserves earlier outstanding obligations.

Director/accountant reminders appear on the welcome page, daily operations and administrative alert panel, and contribute to the alert counter. Unpaid due/overdue reminders persist; upcoming next-month dates can warn before month-end. Open panels refresh on focus and periodically while visible.

Scheduling creates no expenses. Payment opens the expense form with a real payment date and links the expense to the due month; partial payments retain the remaining alert. Users can explicitly link an already recorded expense without duplicating it. Removing or changing a linked expense recalculates the reminder. Variable bills can omit a preset amount and settle on a linked actual expense. No reminders, amounts or due dates were seeded in production.

Configuration is stored in the existing financial payload under `obligations`, available only to director/accountant and included in normal backup/sync. No schema changes. Secretary reads and operational-only saves do not expose or overwrite it. Alerts are inside the app, with no external messaging or automatic expense posting.

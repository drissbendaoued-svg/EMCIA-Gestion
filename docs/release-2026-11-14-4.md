# EMCIA Gestion — 2026.11.14.4

Daily operations now has a sixth KPI card, “تنبيهات أداء اليوم”, alongside the existing five indicators. It shows the unique number of today's scheduled monthly learners with due unpaid balances, plus their total remaining amount. The count uses the same due/discount/payment rules as the daily follow-up list and is independent of list filters. Clicking the card resets the filters and scrolls/focuses the matching list. Payment saves refresh the counter, including changing it to zero after settlement. The existing overall arrears indicator is retained separately.

Validated with DOM integration tests: six cards in the existing three-column grid, unique learner count, filtered-list independence, the real card click handler, and refresh to zero after payment. Existing payment, attendance, finance and reminder regression tests pass. No database changes.
